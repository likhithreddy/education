## Evidence handling
1. Capture the source title, issuing authority, publication/update date, retrieval date, jurisdiction, exact relevant section and a short paraphrase.
2. Track effective/commencement dates separately from publication dates. This matters for the 2025 Indian DPDP framework.
3. Distinguish policy aspirations from enforceable law, and school-directed guidance from directly applicable requirements for a private extracurricular center.
4. Ask qualified Indian counsel to produce an applicability memo before registration, public enrollment or child data processing.
5. Refresh before signing any venue or software vendor contract; the web can change.
6. Keep original statute/policy documents, legal analysis and signed approvals in an access-controlled shared workspace, not the public repository if confidential.
