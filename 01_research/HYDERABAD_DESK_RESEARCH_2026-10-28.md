## What this means for positioning

This evidence **disproves a broad claim that experiential, non-academic or life-skills programs do not exist in Hyderabad**. Several alternatives offer project-based work, social capability, future-ready positioning, home/community delivery or practical play. The unverified gap is *coherent multi-domain progression across a recurring cohort, with accessible child choice and context-specific observations*. This differentiation must be tested with parents and a careful comparison of actual provider experience.

No provider has been contacted; no buyer interviews have been conducted; sample is desk-search convenience; official websites are marketing materials rather than third-party effectiveness evidence. Child-facing operational standards require direct verification.
