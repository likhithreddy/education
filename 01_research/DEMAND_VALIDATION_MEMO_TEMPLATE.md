# Demand validation memo — awaiting interviews
## Minimum evidence
At least 20–30 guardian interviews across multiple neighborhoods and income bands; report recruitment bias. Distinguish statements of interest from deposits or actual purchase behavior.
## Analysis structure
1. Sample composition and selection bias.
2. Frequency of problems mentioned unprompted.
3. Current alternatives and actual spend (self-reported).
4. Travel-time and scheduling constraints.
5. Safety/trust objections.
6. Price acceptance by offer and payment method (not extrapolated as market price).
7. Contradictory or negative feedback.
8. Recommendation: proceed / revise / stop, with uncertainty.
## Quantification
Count coded themes; avoid treating small convenience samples as population estimates. Publish anonymized quotations only with permission.

## Expanded research protocol

## Board-style evidence memo
Begin with one of **Proceed / Redesign / Re-test / Stop**, never an ambiguous “promising” verdict. Specify target child ages, defined Hyderabad catchment, 12-week program fee and intended staffing.

### Required table
| Decision question | Evidence needed | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| Are families underserved? | Past behavior + fair comparison to alternatives | Awaiting interviews |
| Is the offer distinct? | Verified competitor program matrix | Awaiting dated research |
| Will guardians purchase? | Actual paid qualified signups | Not measured |
| Will children enjoy participation? | Child assent, voluntary attendance and feedback | Not measured |
| Is program safe and legal? | Counsel and specialist signed approvals | Not secured |
| Can unit economics work? | Real vendor quotes, staffing and refunds | Illustrative scenarios only |

### Quality requirements
Report every sampling limitation, negative case, refusal, comparison weakness and sensitivity that could reverse the recommendation. Annex the de-identified interview coding summary, evidence register and financial model. Independent reviewer signs a decision with due date; no conversion of untested hypotheses into facts.
