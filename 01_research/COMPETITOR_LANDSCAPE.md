# Competitor and Analogue Landscape — desk-research starting point
**Research status:** Conceptual landscape only. Names and programs require current local validation, pricing checks and site visits; do not describe this as a completed competitive audit.

| Model | Known examples / sources to verify | Core strength | Gap to test (not established fact) |
|---|---|---|---|

| School-adjacent life skills | Indian school clubs and private academies | convenient distribution | whether quality and continuity vary |

## Research questions
1. What currently operates within 30–45 minutes of selected Hyderabad catchments?
2. Which programs cover financial literacy, life skills, social-emotional learning, outdoor skills and real-world simulations?
3. What are verified prices, formats, age bands, class sizes, staff qualifications and parent reviews?
4. What outcomes are demonstrated versus claimed?
5. What are the operational/real-estate costs and local willingness-to-pay segments?
