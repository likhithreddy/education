# 12-Week Pilot Curriculum
**Format:** 3 hours/week including breaks, transition and child-led play. Activities must be reviewed for accessibility, allergies, supervision and developmental appropriateness.

| Week | Theme | Core experience | Domains |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Belonging & trust | team charter; choice-based games | communication, social, character |
| 2 | Understanding emotions | feelings cards; setback role-play | emotional, communication |
| 3 | Money in everyday life | needs/wants and shopping simulation | financial, critical thinking |
| 4 | Plan a household budget | budget tradeoff challenge | financial, practical |
| 5 | Food & hygiene | no-heat snack preparation with allergy protocol | practical, physical |
| 6 | Safety & helping | age-safe first-aid awareness; help-seeking scenarios | physical, character |
| 7 | Build & iterate | recycled-material design challenge | creativity, critical thinking |
| 8 | Speak & collaborate | small-group pitch and active listening | communication, social |
| 9 | Digital & AI judgment | fact-check an AI answer; privacy game | digital, critical thinking |
| 10 | Community mapping | identify local accessibility/environment issue | citizenship, empathy |
| 11 | Capstone build | teams prototype a small community solution | agency, creativity, teamwork |
| 12 | Showcase & reflection | child-led demonstration; family feedback | all domains |

## Standard session flow
- 00:00–00:15 welcome, emotional check-in, consent/choice reminder
- 00:15–00:35 warm-up/game
- 00:35–01:20 hands-on challenge A
- 01:20–01:35 break, water, accessibility needs
- 01:35–02:20 hands-on challenge B
- 02:20–02:45 reflection, peer collaboration, optional presentation
- 02:45–03:00 cleanup, safe handover to approved guardian

## Facilitator preparation
Prepare materials, safety check, alternative activities, visual instructions, emergency kit, attendance register, authorized pickup list, child-accessible feedback method.

## Pilot capstone
Teams select a community problem and create a mock-up, poster, prototype or demonstration; no real public commerce or travel required.
