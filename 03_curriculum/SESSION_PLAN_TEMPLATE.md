## Field-ready detail and acceptance criteria

## Implementation-ready per-session specification
- Session ID, version, author, qualified reviewer, participant age band and staffing assumptions.
- Three observable learning behaviors, each mapped to dimensions.
- Context and exact scenario materials, safe alternatives and prerequisite permissions.
- Timetable in 10–40-minute blocks, including supervised breaks and handover.
- Facilitator script for opening, main challenge, optional variation and reflection.
- Sample prompts, predictable misconceptions and non-coercive redirection.
- Physical/psychosocial/digital hazards, mitigation, stop rules and emergency escalation.
- Accessibility and alternative modes without isolation or shame.
- What observations to capture, which identifiers must be excluded, privacy retention.
- Optional no-cost family reflection and post-session improvement log.
- Sign-offs: developmental, safeguarding, program lead; date and next review.

**Completion test:** an independently trained facilitator can run a safe rehearsal without needing author explanation.
