# Activity risk assessments — draft, requires local sign-off
| Activity | Hazard | Initial concern | Control | Stop trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Group games | Falls, exclusion | Medium | Clear floor, seated options, no contact | Injury/distress |
| Mock money | Shaming about household wealth | Medium | Fictional budgets only | Personal disclosure |
| Snack preparation | Allergies, contamination | High | No-heat, guardian allergy review, approved ingredients, hand hygiene | Symptoms/unknown allergen |
| Safety role-play | Fear, triggering scenarios | Medium | Age-appropriate fictional cases; opt-out | Distress |
| Craft prototypes | Cuts, ingestion | Medium | Blunt tools, inspect materials, supervise | Unsafe item |
| AI judgment | Exposure of personal data | Medium | Offline fictional prompts, no child accounts | Identifiable data entry |
| Showcase | Consent and performance pressure | Medium | Voluntary, no filming by default | Unwanted recording |
| Arrival/pickup | Unauthorized collection | High | Guardian ID, approved list, two-adult handover | Identity mismatch |

**Before use:** local safeguarding lead and qualified professionals must review risks, staff-to-child ratios, food safety, first-aid readiness and emergency response. This draft is not a certification.
