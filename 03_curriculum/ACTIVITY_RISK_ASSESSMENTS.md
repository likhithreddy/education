**Before use:** local safeguarding lead and qualified professionals must review risks, staff-to-child ratios, food safety, first-aid readiness and emergency response. This draft is not a certification.

## Quality expansion — actionable protocols

## Hazard register methodology
For each activity record: hazards, exposed persons, conditions increasing exposure, prevention, safe staffing, emergency supplies, residual risk, owner, reviewer and stop threshold. Use scenario-based rehearsal, not only a signed form.

**Food simulation:** prefer fictional or no-food versions. If real food is used, record guardian-approved allergen controls, safe handling, sanitation and trained oversight; stop if an unknown allergy risk emerges.
**Movement:** offer seated alternatives, safe flooring, adequate space, hydration and no contact races; stop for pain or distress.
**Making:** use blunt, non-toxic materials, adult inspection and accessible substitutes. No heat, knives, electricity or dangerous chemicals.
**Digital learning:** use offline fictional examples, no child accounts or personal data entry.
**Visitor/capstone:** roster and verify adults, supervise all access and honor separate media consent.
**Psychosocial:** avoid forced confessions, public shaming, competitions over financial circumstances or personal histories.

A qualified local reviewer must verify hazards for the actual venue, participants and materials. Store confidential incident reports outside GitHub.
