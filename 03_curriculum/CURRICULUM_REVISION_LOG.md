## Change-control protocol
Record: revision ID; session/version; feedback source and consent; reason; old activity; new activity; risk change; inclusion impact; assessment comparability; reviewer; approval; effective week.

**Examples of controlled updates:** simplify a complex budget prompt for accessibility, introduce visual instructions, replace a sensory-overloading activity, correct factual content, or add supervised quiet-room option. Do not silently revise scored tasks after seeing results.

**Safety-critical change:** stop the affected activity until a competent reviewer approves mitigation. **Post-pilot:** distinguish planned original curriculum from adaptations informed by authentic child feedback; no claim that feedback has been collected before sessions occur.
