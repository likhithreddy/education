- Contains purpose, intended user and decisions supported.
- Has concrete steps, examples, acceptance criteria, owner and evidence requirements.
- Clearly marks assumptions and illustrative values.
- Includes dependencies and child protection/privacy boundaries where applicable.
- Is usable without an unstated interpretation by its author.
- Has named reviewer, last review date, next validation action and sign-off when operational.
- Is cross-consistent with the 81-task master register and other documents.

## Highest-priority remaining gaps

1. **Safeguarding and law (critical):** review real permissions, staff screening, incident reporting, independent safeguarding lead, consent language, insurance and venue emergency readiness. No child-facing launch without these sign-offs.

2. **Actual market evidence (high):** the competitor matrix has no verified local provider quotes and the parent interview log has no field data. Do not write a validation memo based on invented answers.

3. **Curriculum quality (high):** 12 session guides now include activity-specific scenarios, timings and stop rules, but need practical rehearsal, child-development expert review, localized examples and materials prototyping.

4. **Finances (high):** all figures are illustrative; reconcile fixed and variable assumptions in one canonical model and replace with written vendor quotations. Validate capacities and refunds before revenue commitments.

5. **Software and data (high):** the website is a static no-data concept. Consent, privacy, secure adult-only interest intake, payment, authorization and retention must be implemented and tested before public collection.

6. **Evaluation (high):** the tools measure observed behavior within tasks and feasibility, not clinical attributes or causal long-term impact. No baseline, feedback or final report can be truthfully populated before the pilot.

7. **Editorial consistency (medium):** new implementation sections were added to draft documents for traceability; a full copyedit/consolidation is needed to remove overlapping instructions, reconcile costs, and ensure one source of truth.

## Next-quality-pass acceptance
Independent reviewer rehearses **every** session, tests venue/child handoff and consent pathways, reconciles one canonical priced budget, provides signed issue log, and verifies each document's external citations and definitions. Record closed issues and reopen on any material change.
