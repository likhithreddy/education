Life Readiness Lab · A child development concept
Hyderabad · Research and design phase

Skills for life.
Room to grow.

Exploring playful, supervised, real-world experiences for children aged 9–12—building everyday judgment, independence, relationships and agency without another exam.

Not accepting enrollment yet

What children could explore

Think and decide

Budget a pretend café, compare real-world choices, test ideas and ask better questions.

Connect and contribute

Practice listening, working with others, respectful disagreement and community design.

Live safely and independently

Explore practical routines, help-seeking, digital safety and age-appropriate health habits.

Proposed learning approach

Twelve weekly sessions of approximately three hours each. Children learn through safe role-play, simple prototypes, cooperative projects and optional reflection. Activities will include accessible alternatives, supervised breaks and meaningful opportunities to opt out.

Not: clinical therapy, school replacement, standardized personality scoring, guaranteed future success or unsupervised AI use.

Questions families should ask

Is this another tuition class?
No. Activities focus on applied life experiences.
Will children be ranked?
No. Facilitators will use descriptive, task-specific observations where approved.
Are fees, venue or start dates confirmed?
No. These depend on market research, staffing and independent safety/legal approvals.
Can my child decline an activity?
Yes. Safe, respectful alternatives are integral to the model.

Current status

This is a research prototype. We are not collecting contact details, child information, payments or applications through this page. The program must complete independent developmental, legal, venue and safeguarding checks before operating.
