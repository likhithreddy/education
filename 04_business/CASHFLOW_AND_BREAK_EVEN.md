# Pilot cash flow and break-even — illustrative, not verified pricing
## Inputs
Fixed direct program cost ₹110,000; variable cost ₹1,400 per enrolled child; program fee ₹9,000; no tax/overhead/capex/refund modeled. These are assumptions to be replaced with vendor quotes.
## Equations
Revenue = N × fee. Direct cost = fixed + N × variable. Contribution = N × (fee − variable) − fixed. Break-even seats = ceiling(fixed/(fee − variable)) = **15** (assuming every seat is paid in full).
## Cash timing
| Stage | Cash inflow | Cash outflow |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch | Deposits only if legally approved | Venue deposits, background checks, insurance, printing |
| Week 1 | Remaining fees | Staff, supplies |
| Weeks 2–12 | None assumed | Staff, consumables, utilities |
| Post-pilot | None assumed | Refunds, reporting, closeout |

**Controls:** retain contingency; no nonrefundable commitments until founder approval; track actual invoices weekly; model tax, payment fees, discounts, refunds, scholarships and utilization before pricing decision.
**Updated model status:** scenario calculations complete; final cashflow BLOCKED by actual quotes and payment terms.
