# Illustrative Pilot Economics — INR
**Scenario only; not vendor-quoted costs.** 12 weeks, 20 children, 3h/week.

| Item | Base assumption |
|---|---:|
| Fee per child | ₹9,000 |
| Enrollment | 20 |
| Revenue | ₹180,000 |
| Facilitator compensation | ₹48,000 |
| Venue hire | ₹36,000 |
| Materials & consumables | ₹18,000 |
| Safeguarding, first aid, staff training allocation | ₹12,000 |
| Marketing | ₹12,000 |
| Admin, payment and communication | ₹9,000 |
| Contingency | ₹15,000 |
| Total direct pilot costs | ₹150,000 |
| Contribution before central overhead, tax, founder time and capex | ₹30,000 |

Break-even at ₹9,000 per child: ceil(₹150,000 / ₹9,000) = **17 enrollments**, assuming all costs fixed. This oversimplifies per-child variable costs; revise after vendor quotes. At 12 enrollments, contribution = **−₹42,000**; at 24 enrollments, **₹66,000** before extra staffing/capacity cost. **Do not increase enrollment beyond safe venue/staffing capacity.**

## Commercial work
Get 3 venue quotes, 3 facilitator quotes, insurance quotes, materials bills, marketing costs, payment fees, refund liability and GST/accounting advice. Calculate fully loaded cost, cash runway and cancellation downside.

## Pricing test
Test ₹4k/₹8k/₹12k/₹16k options with clear comparable program description; record willingness-to-pay evidence separately from actual payment.

## Quality expansion — actionable protocols

## Unit economics definitions
**Gross revenue:** paid enrollments × realized fee, less discounts, refunds and payment taxes/fees as appropriate. **Variable cost per child:** consumables, payment processing, additional staffing and per-head insurance if applicable. **Fixed per cohort:** venue, minimum facilitator staffing, training, administration and safety baseline. **Contribution:** revenue minus direct cohort costs. **Operating profit** also deducts central overhead, marketing, licensing, amortized startup cost and taxes.

**Sample only:** 20 children at ₹9,000 yields ₹180,000 gross, and assumed ₹135,000 direct cost yields ₹45,000 contribution before omitted costs. This is not a market quote or credible forecast without a real vendor ledger.

**Sensitivity:** calculate a 3×3 grid on enrollment (10/15/20) and realized fees (₹6k/₹9k/₹12k), then run separate high/low venue and staffing cases. Do not exceed the safe capacity purely to reach break-even.

**CAC and retention:** calculate channel-specific cash outlay divided by confirmed paid families, record refunds, attendance and cohort re-enrollment intentions separately. Family referrals are not free if incentives or staff time are involved.

**Financial gate:** signed spend ceiling, reserve for refunds, and cash-flow timing consistent with deposits and vendor payments.
