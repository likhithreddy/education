# Illustrative Pilot Economics — INR
**Scenario only; not vendor-quoted costs.** 12 weeks, 20 children, 3h/week.

| Item | Base assumption |
|---|---:|
| Fee per child | ₹9,000 |
| Enrollment | 20 |
| Revenue | ₹180,000 |
| Facilitator compensation | ₹48,000 |
| Venue hire | ₹36,000 |
| Materials & consumables | ₹18,000 |
| Safeguarding, first aid, staff training allocation | ₹12,000 |
| Marketing | ₹12,000 |
| Admin, payment and communication | ₹9,000 |
| Contingency | ₹15,000 |
| Total direct pilot costs | ₹150,000 |
| Contribution before central overhead, tax, founder time and capex | ₹30,000 |

Break-even at ₹9,000 per child: ceil(₹150,000 / ₹9,000) = **17 enrollments**, assuming all costs fixed. This oversimplifies per-child variable costs; revise after vendor quotes. At 12 enrollments, contribution = **−₹42,000**; at 24 enrollments, **₹66,000** before extra staffing/capacity cost. **Do not increase enrollment beyond safe venue/staffing capacity.**

## Commercial work
Get 3 venue quotes, 3 facilitator quotes, insurance quotes, materials bills, marketing costs, payment fees, refund liability and GST/accounting advice. Calculate fully loaded cost, cash runway and cancellation downside.

## Pricing test
Test ₹4k/₹8k/₹12k/₹16k options with clear comparable program description; record willingness-to-pay evidence separately from actual payment.
