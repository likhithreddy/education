# Go-to-Market and Positioning
## Positioning
A 12-week, hands-on life-capability lab for ages 9–12: children practice real-world decision-making, teamwork, financial basics, creativity and everyday independence through guided experiences.
## Avoid
'Guaranteed leadership', 'perfect child', 'IQ boost', 'replaces school', 'AI-proof career', 'clinically proven transformation' without credible evidence.
## Pilot segments to test
Parents seeking structured weekend activities; school parent groups; residential communities; community centers. Compare convenience, price sensitivity and safety expectations.
## Funnel
Discovery interview → waitlist with explicit consent → orientation/safety FAQ → trial open house → enrollment → onboarding → attendance → parent feedback → referral (optional).
## Trust signals
Visible staff qualifications, child protection policy, emergency plan, transparent fees/refunds, realistic outcomes, accessible activities, parent visit opportunities.

## Acquisition experiment with ethical messaging
**Value message:** real-world challenges in small facilitated groups, age-appropriate experiences, safe choice and family reflection. Do not promise future employability, intelligence enhancement, cure of social anxiety or guaranteed confidence.

**Sequence:** 1) identify catchment and parent access; 2) run non-leading interviews; 3) host free information demonstration only after safety approval; 4) present clear price, location and refund policy; 5) record child interest and guardian consent; 6) enroll paid participants; 7) report reasons for withdrawal.

**Channel comparison:** school/community referral, residential association, parent network, local events, targeted digital campaign. Track spend, leads, attended briefings, eligible families, paid conversion and refund requests by source. Do not harvest school directories or distribute child-identifying marketing lists.

**Proof:** at least one externally recruited cohort with actual paid commitments; separate interests from conversion. If demand is weak, revisit the need rather than intensifying manipulative sales claims.
