# Go-to-Market and Positioning
## Positioning
A 12-week, hands-on life-capability lab for ages 9–12: children practice real-world decision-making, teamwork, financial basics, creativity and everyday independence through guided experiences.
## Avoid
'Guaranteed leadership', 'perfect child', 'IQ boost', 'replaces school', 'AI-proof career', 'clinically proven transformation' without credible evidence.
## Pilot segments to test
Parents seeking structured weekend activities; school parent groups; residential communities; community centers. Compare convenience, price sensitivity and safety expectations.
## Funnel
Discovery interview → waitlist with explicit consent → orientation/safety FAQ → trial open house → enrollment → onboarding → attendance → parent feedback → referral (optional).
## Trust signals
Visible staff qualifications, child protection policy, emergency plan, transparent fees/refunds, realistic outcomes, accessible activities, parent visit opportunities.
