# Gates and Milestones
| Gate | Indicative week | Required evidence | Decision owner |
|---|---:|---|---|
| G0 Concept alignment | 1 | Charter, scope, assumptions | Founder |
| G1 Demand validation | 4 | 25–40 guardian interviews, 5–10 educator interviews, competitor checks, documented willingness to pay | Founder |
| G2 Program validation | 8 | Specialist-reviewed curriculum, trial activities, accessible design, safety review | Founder + specialists |
| G3 Launch authorization | 11 | Legal clearance, safe venue, staffing, insurance, emergency plan, parent forms, enrollment floor | Founder + safeguarding lead |
| G4 Mid-pilot checkpoint | 17 | Attendance, child feedback, incident trends, budget | Founder + program lead |
| G5 Pilot decision | 24 | Results, unit economics, child/parent feedback, safety audit | Founder |

**Rule:** Failure of child safety, legal or safeguarding gate = no launch regardless of sales.
