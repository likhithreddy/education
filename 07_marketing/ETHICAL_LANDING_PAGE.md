# Landing page copy — concept validation (not an enrollment offer)
## Hero
**Life skills for a changing world.**
Hands-on, age-appropriate experiences that help children practice collaboration, everyday decision-making, creativity and independence.
**Join the parent research waitlist** (interest only; no payment or guaranteed seat).
## How it works
A proposed 12-week pilot for ages 9–12 in Hyderabad: one supervised three-hour session per week, practical group challenges and optional family reflection.
## What children explore
Money choices, communication, food and hygiene, safety awareness, creativity, digital judgment and community problem-solving.
## Our approach
Playful practice, inclusive participation, no rankings and no promises of career or IQ outcomes.
## FAQ
**Does it replace school?** No. It is a proposed complementary experience.
**Is it a certified course?** No certification is currently claimed.
**Will AI interact directly with my child?** Not in the proposed initial pilot; mentors use pre-reviewed materials.
**What is the fee?** Not finalized; pricing research is underway.
**When and where?** To be confirmed after venue and safeguarding approvals.
**What happens after I express interest?** We may contact you for an optional research conversation. No enrollment commitment.
## Privacy
Collect guardian contact details only; do not collect children's names or health information on the public waitlist. Provide explicit consent and deletion contact.
