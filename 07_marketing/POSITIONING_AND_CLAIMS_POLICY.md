# Positioning and responsible claims
**Position:** experiential life-readiness enrichment for children, not school replacement, therapy or certification.
**Permitted language:** 'designed to help children practice', 'pilot', 'hands-on experiences', 'parent feedback collected'.
**Not permitted without credible evidence:** 'proven to increase IQ', 'guaranteed confidence', 'makes children future-proof', 'prevents mental health problems', 'world's first', 'scientifically validated outcomes'.
**Child privacy:** no names, identifiable photos, testimonials or work products in marketing without specific informed guardian permission and appropriate child assent.
**AI claims:** AI may support internal lesson planning; do not imply child-facing AI tutoring if absent.
**Review:** founder and safeguarding lead approve all public claims before release.
