# Parent-Facing Pilot Concept Card
## Life Readiness Lab — 12-week exploratory pilot
For children aged 9–12. Weekly 3-hour guided sessions using practical projects, games and group challenges to explore money, communication, teamwork, safety, creativity, digital judgment and community participation.

**What children do:** plan a mock budget, build a prototype, practice safe everyday tasks, test claims made by AI, and present a child-chosen group project.

**What this is not:** academic tuition, therapy, daycare, a certified qualification or a promise to make every child excel at everything.

**Safeguarding:** trained adults, supervised activities, guardian pickup, consent, privacy safeguards, emergency protocols. Final staff/venue arrangements will be disclosed before enrollment.

**Status:** research concept; dates, venue, fee and availability are not confirmed.

## Implementation expansion (requires review)

## Parent communication playbook
**Initial inquiry:** “We're researching a 12-week hands-on life-readiness experience for ages 9–12. The program is a supervised pilot, not a school substitute or a guaranteed developmental intervention. What would be useful for your child and what concerns would you have?”

**Orientation:** explain activities, timetable, contact details, specialist-reviewed safeguards, consent choices, refund conditions, accessibility options and child opt-out process.

**Weekly update template:** “This week children explored [scenario]. They tried [observable action], considered [trade-off], and had an optional home reflection: [question]. Your child's individual observations are available privately upon request, never in group comparisons.”

**Complaint escalation:** acknowledge promptly without defensiveness, provide safeguarding lead/alternative contact, keep details private and provide a written update under a reviewed timetable.

**Media:** use child work only with distinct and revocable consent; no implicit consent from attendance or group chat membership.

## Expanded operating instructions

## Parent communication scripts and escalation
**Research outreach:** “We're studying hands-on life-readiness experiences for children 9–12. We're looking for what families already do and what is missing. This is voluntary research, not an enrollment offer.”

**Orientation:** give the actual venue, staffing, schedule, age limits, safety and privacy contacts, optional activity choices, refunds, incident escalation and accessible participation arrangements.

**Weekly update:** describe the fictional challenge, skills practiced and one concrete example, not a permanent child label or numerical development score. An optional family reflection should require no paid materials.

**Complaint route:** confirm receipt, provide safeguarding officer or alternate independent contact, document actions confidentially and follow qualified reporting obligations. Never discuss one child's sensitive information in group chats.

**Media:** images require separate child-sensitive consent and must not appear as proof of developmental outcomes.
