## Detailed implementation supplement

## Definitions and interpretation
**Paid conversion:** actual enrolled paying guardians divided by uniquely eligible qualified enquiries. **Attendance:** delivered sessions attended divided by sessions available to each participant; cancelled sessions reported separately. **Completion:** participants meeting the pre-agreed attendance threshold divided by all enrolled participants. **Cost per child-hour:** direct cohort expenses divided by total actual attended child-hours. **Contribution:** realized revenue net of discounts/refunds less direct cohort costs.

Separate self-report (“parent believes child is more confident”) from directly observed demonstration (“child explained two options in a budget simulation”). Missing observations, child choice to opt out, practice effect, sample selection and staff expectations must be documented. No global “whole child percentage”.

**Decision dashboard:** enrolled/target, attendance, child-experience feedback, parent response rate, incident status (aggregated), refund rate, cash contribution, facilitator hours and accessibility adjustments. Present failures and limits alongside strengths.
