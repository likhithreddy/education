## Field-ready detail and acceptance criteria

## Week-six operations report
**Header:** cohort ID, location, dates, reviewer, consent version and safeguarding lead.
**Delivery:** 6 planned vs completed sessions, facilitator changes, participant attendance trend, supplies and budget variance.
**Experience:** anonymized child choices and enjoyment, activities declined, inclusion/access needs met and missed.
**Safety:** aggregate incidents and corrective actions, confidential escalations excluded.
**Parents:** response denominator, detailed concerns and scheduling/price obstacles.
**Decision:** maintain, modify or suspend—with specific changes, owner and deadline.

Avoid comparing children by raw numeric “development scores.” Record revisions so baseline/endline evidence remains interpretable.
