# Pilot Execution Runbook
## T−14 to T−1 days
Confirm gate G3; final enrollment list; guardian briefing; staff drills; safe materials; venue inspection; accessibility accommodations; contacts and pickup; refund policy; incident escalation; baseline child/guardian input.
## Every session
- Before: venue check, staffing, emergency kit, attendance list, adaptations.
- Arrival: guardian handover, attendance, child choice reminder.
- Delivery: follow session plan; monitor safety and engagement; breaks.
- Departure: verify authorized pickup; record incident/near miss; secure materials/data.
- After: 15-minute staff debrief; child feedback (voluntary); adjust next session.
## Weekly metrics
Enrolled, attended, retained, satisfaction/engagement feedback, incidents and near misses, hours delivered, staff coverage, actual costs, complaints, accessibility issues.
## Week 6 checkpoint
Review safety, attendance, child enjoyment, parent feedback and costs; make adjustments or pause if needed.
## Week 12 close
Child-led showcase; portfolio sharing with guardians; optional endline interviews; vendor and staff retrospective; final financial report.
## Post-pilot
Decision memo: continue/modify/stop, supported by evidence and limitations. No claims of causal improvement without suitable evaluation design.

## Detailed implementation supplement

## Pilot command center
**Week –2:** verify independent child-development review, trained/verified facilitators, venue hazard inspection, accessible adaptations, consent records, insurance and emergency drill. **Week –1:** family orientation, child assent, final program rehearsal and approved pickup setup.

**Each week:** staffing and materials confirmation → hazard sweep → secure check-in → supervised challenge and breaks → optional reflection → confidential incidents → authorized release → operational debrief → finance variance review. Assign a named lead and backup.

**Week 6:** check attendance/engagement, accessibility, child complaints, parent response and cost trend. Record any changed activities so endline comparisons are interpretable.

**Week 12:** optional capstone, child-selected artifacts, private developmental observations, guardian feedback and full cost reconciliation. Do not describe repeated observed performance as lifelong change.

**Immediate suspension:** unsafe staffing, severe safeguarding allegation unresolved, material venue danger, unlawful operation or missing essential guardian authorization. The safeguarding lead may suspend independently of commercial concerns.
