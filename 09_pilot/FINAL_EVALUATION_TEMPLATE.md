# Pilot evaluation — awaiting week 12
## Questions
Was the pilot safe and inclusive? Did children engage voluntarily? Were practical skills observed? Would guardians recommend or repurchase? Were delivery costs sustainable? What failed?
## Metrics
Enrollment, attendance, completion, safety incidents, child-reported enjoyment, guardian feedback, facilitator observations, repeat interest, actual cash collected, direct cost per child, refunds, complaints.
## Interpretation
No causal learning claims without appropriate evaluation design. Separate observed change from parent perception and marketing claims. Report missing data and negative findings.
## Decision
Scale / repeat pilot / redesign / stop, with documented founder and safeguarding approvals.
**Status:** BLOCKED until pilot completion.

## Field-ready detail and acceptance criteria

## Evidence dossier to complete after the pilot
1. Date, location, staff, age bands, signed approvals and actually delivered sessions.
2. Recruitment funnel with unique denominators and confirmed paid enrollment.
3. Attendance by session, child opt-outs, withdrawals and missing records.
4. Observed applied capabilities with concrete examples, prompting and accommodation context.
5. Child voice and guardian survey results, negative feedback and response bias.
6. Safeguarding incidents, aggregate resolution and any stop-work event (restricted details stored separately).
7. Budget-versus-actual expenses, refunds, tax treatment reviewed by accountant, and unit economics.
8. Design deviations, staff workload and accessibility barriers.
9. Plausible alternate explanations, including selection and practice effects.
10. Decision: stop, redesign, repeat, partner or scale—with evidence and accountable signature.

**Do not populate hypothetical outcomes or treat a blank report as completed evaluation.**
