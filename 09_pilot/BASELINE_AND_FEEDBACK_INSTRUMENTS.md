# Baseline and feedback instruments — pilot draft
## Guardian intake (optional, 5 minutes)
1. Which real-life skills does your child enjoy practicing? (open)
2. What would you like them to try more often? (open)
3. What would make this program safe and accessible for your family? (open)
4. How much travel time is manageable? (ranges)
5. May we contact you about your experience? (separate consent)
## Child check-in (opt-in, age appropriate)
Choose an emoji or words: 'How comfortable do you feel trying new activities today?' 'What would you like to learn?' Child may skip any question.
## Facilitator observation
Session/date, coded child ID, activity, factual observed behavior, support offered, context, child's own reflection; 'not observed' allowed. No diagnosis, rankings or intelligence inference.
## Weekly pulse
Child: favorite/least favorite activity and one suggested change (optional). Guardian: logistical feedback, perceived value and safety concerns (separate urgent reporting channel).
## Exit
Child: 'What would you do differently now?' Guardian: perceived changes, dissatisfaction, willingness to re-enroll (not equal to purchase), price feedback.
## Data safeguards
Collect minimum data; protect identifiers separately; set retention schedule with legal review; avoid public sharing of individual scores.
