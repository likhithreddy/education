# Guardian enrollment packet — draft for legal/safeguarding review
## Program details
Program: 12-week life readiness pilot; 9–12 years; three hours weekly; venue/date and staffing to be inserted after approval.
## Required fields
Child preferred name; guardian name and contact; secondary emergency contact; authorized pickup adults; accessibility needs; allergies and relevant emergency instructions; preferred language; consent to participate; signed acknowledgement of safety rules.
## Separate optional consents (unchecked by default)
[ ] Use de-identified feedback for internal evaluation. [ ] Child work display with guardian review. [ ] Photography/media (specific use, retention, revocation). [ ] Optional digital service (specific data and provider).
## Emergency authorization
Insert local-law-reviewed language covering emergency contact order, ambulance access, care decisions and who bears costs. Do not use an unreviewed blanket medical consent.
## Privacy notice requirements
Controller identity, purpose, data categories, retention period, access controls, processor details, contact channel, correction/deletion request process, withdrawal and complaint process. Obtain Indian privacy counsel review.
## Conduct and complaints
Two-adult safeguarding standard where feasible; no private messaging with children; accessible reporting channel; designated safeguarding lead and escalation to authorities as required.
## Signatures
Guardian signature/date; staff verifier/date; separate optional consents recorded with timestamp.
**Not ready for live use until reviewed for local law, child protection and data protection.**

## Implementation expansion (requires review)

## Reviewable enrollment package structure
Section 1: plain-language scope, dates, activity types, age eligibility, accessibility contact, fee/refund terms and who supervises children.
Section 2: minimum guardian identity and contact data, emergency contact, authorized pickup and approved change procedure; store in restricted production systems.
Section 3: relevant care accommodations, dietary allergy/medical assistance information on a necessity basis only; consulted with qualified professionals.
Section 4: explicit participation consent with risks and emergency response acknowledgement as reviewed by counsel.
Section 5: separate optional consents for photography, research and communications. Refusal does not compromise core participation.
Section 6: child assent/ability to opt out, guardian questions, complaint and incident escalation path.
Section 7: retention, access, correction and deletion process under applicable Indian privacy requirements.
Section 8: signature, version, date, witness/verification and secure audit trail.

**Critical:** this packet is not a substitute for enforceable terms. Local lawyer and safeguarding reviewer must approve wording and applicable rules before use. Completed forms must never be committed to GitHub.
