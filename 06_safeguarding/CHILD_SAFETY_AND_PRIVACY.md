# Child Safeguarding and Privacy — DRAFT FOR PROFESSIONAL REVIEW
**Not legal advice. Do not use operationally without qualified local review.**
## Non-negotiables
Child dignity, non-discrimination, safe supervision, staff boundaries, mandatory reporting compliance, consent/assent, no corporal punishment, no humiliating rankings, no private staff-child communication.
## India-specific legal review checklist
Ask local counsel to review applicable POCSO duties and reporting; relevant Telangana establishment/local permissions; employment and background-check requirements; fire/occupancy permissions; food handling rules where applicable; insurance; consumer refunds; India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework and applicable rules/commencement dates. Do not assume all provisions are in force without verification.
## Guardian enrollment packet
Program details, schedule, risk disclosures, emergency contacts, authorized pickup persons, allergies/accessibility needs (minimal necessary), consent for participation, optional separately revocable media consent, refund terms, complaint channel.
## Incident handling
1. Make child safe and obtain emergency medical help when needed.
2. Preserve factual contemporaneous notes; do not interrogate the child.
3. Escalate promptly to safeguarding lead and required authorities under applicable law.
4. Inform guardians when appropriate and legally permitted; preserve confidentiality.
5. Log follow-up, corrective actions and restrictions on staff access.
## Privacy
Collect minimum necessary data; no biometric/face recognition; no default video recording; no direct child AI chatbot; strict staff access; deletion schedule; guardian rights process; vendor review; incident response.
## AI
AI may generate activity drafts for adult review. Never upload identifiable child narratives, medical information, images or behavioral profiles to public AI services without a lawful, specifically reviewed basis and appropriate safeguards.

## Quality expansion — implementation requirements

## Mandatory pre-launch specialist review

This is a **draft control framework**, not legal clearance. Engage a qualified Indian legal professional and child safeguarding specialist to verify the program's exact duties, site permissions, screening, first aid coverage, incident notification, guardian consents and insurance. Create an independent escalation route if the designated safeguarding lead is implicated.

Minimum measures: no isolated staff-child interaction; approved and recorded authorized pickup; trained and screened personnel; visible supervision during breaks; procedures for alleged misconduct; objective incident documentation stored in restricted systems; no child-facing unsupervised AI; non-coercive child participation and accessibility accommodations.

**Digital Personal Data Protection framework:** central government notified staged commencements in November 2025. Counsel must check which duties are in force on the launch date, relevant child-data obligations, consent, notices, retention and breach response. Source: https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf and rules https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf . Do not assume all rules were effective immediately.

**Child protection source:** NCPCR guidelines library at https://ncpcr.gov.in/ncpcr_new/public/guidelines . Verify which school guidelines are directly applicable to a non-school activity center instead of treating them as automatic licenses.

**Stop conditions:** no verified screening, no safe supervision, no authorized pickup process, unreviewed food/movement hazards, unresolved safeguarding report, inaccessible emergency exit, or missing mandatory approval. Any one stops launch.
