## Interview metadata and evidence integrity
Fields: interview ID (not name), recruitment channel, date, parent/guardian eligibility, approximate age band, geography, current alternatives, spontaneous needs, price stated before concept, reaction after concept, child interest signal, objections, consent to anonymized notes, researcher and verification status.

**Interviewer note discipline:** mark exact respondent quotes separately from paraphrase; avoid leading questions and avoid identifying child details. Store contact follow-up consents separately in restricted storage.

**Analysis review:** report how many participants declined, why, and why sample may not represent Hyderabad families. No invented interview content.
