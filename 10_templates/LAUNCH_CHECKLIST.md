# Launch Authorization Checklist
- [ ] Legal review completed and written requirements documented
- [ ] Child safeguarding policy approved and staff trained
- [ ] Lawful staff vetting and references completed
- [ ] Venue/fire/accessibility inspection passed
- [ ] First-aid and emergency drill completed
- [ ] Staff coverage and substitute plan confirmed
- [ ] Guardian consent, pickup and emergency details secured
- [ ] Allergy/food and activity risk assessments completed
- [ ] Insurance coverage confirmed
- [ ] Data privacy/retention controls verified
- [ ] Fees, refunds, cancellation terms approved
- [ ] Enrollment minimum and budget reserve approved
- [ ] Child-development specialist curriculum sign-off
- [ ] Founder written go/no-go decision

## Field-ready detail and acceptance criteria

## Launch gate sign-off
| Domain | Verification artifact | Decision owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Child developmental fit | Signed curriculum review | Qualified specialist | Pending |
| Safeguarding | Training, screening, code of conduct, reporting | Safeguarding lead | Pending |
| Premises | Fire/exit/room/accessibility inspection | Local competent reviewer | Pending |
| Legal | License/registration/tax/data compliance memo | Indian counsel | Pending |
| Insurance | Appropriate policy & scope confirmation | Founder/broker | Pending |
| Enrollment | Valid consent and pickup authority | Operations | Pending |
| Staff | Training drill and backup coverage | Program lead | Pending |
| Finance | Spending cap, refund reserve, signed quotes | Founder | Pending |
| Experience | Full facilitator rehearsal | Developmental lead | Pending |
| Emergency | Drill and communications | Safeguarding lead | Pending |

A single unresolved critical requirement means **NO-GO**. Record dated approval, link to restricted evidence and expiry/review date. This checklist is not itself a certification.
