# Family participation boundaries
## Principles
Participation is optional and non-punitive; no child should be disadvantaged by guardian time, income, literacy or household circumstances.
## Weekly contact
Send a 150-word neutral recap, one optional 10-minute conversation prompt and an accessible alternative. Never demand photos or home financial details.
## Communication
Guardian receives information about their own child only. No public comparison, leaderboards, coercive referrals, or advertising using child stories.
## Escalation
Safety concerns are handled privately by safeguarding lead under approved policy, not in a parent group chat.
## Consent
Separate, revocable choices for program participation, emergency care process, media, anonymized evaluation and optional technology tools.

## Quality expansion — actionable protocols

## Parent participation as a low-pressure partnership
Every week send one short summary, two genuine questions, and one optional household experience requiring no purchase. Activities are invitations, never graded homework; families may decline without disadvantage.

**Orientation:** explain program boundaries, child choice, approved communication channels, photo permissions, safety contacts and the limitations of developmental observations.

**Midpoint feedback:** give examples of observable situations rather than claims such as “your child became a leader.” Invite the child to correct adults' interpretations, with voluntary family involvement.

**Equity:** provide bilingual or translated summaries as required; do not assume caregivers have spare time, internet, or supplies. Never pressure disclosure of family finances, health, conflict or religious beliefs.

**Privacy:** do not distribute cohort chat lists with personal phone numbers or images without independent authorization.
