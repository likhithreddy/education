# Family participation boundaries
## Principles
Participation is optional and non-punitive; no child should be disadvantaged by guardian time, income, literacy or household circumstances.
## Weekly contact
Send a 150-word neutral recap, one optional 10-minute conversation prompt and an accessible alternative. Never demand photos or home financial details.
## Communication
Guardian receives information about their own child only. No public comparison, leaderboards, coercive referrals, or advertising using child stories.
## Escalation
Safety concerns are handled privately by safeguarding lead under approved policy, not in a parent group chat.
## Consent
Separate, revocable choices for program participation, emergency care process, media, anonymized evaluation and optional technology tools.
