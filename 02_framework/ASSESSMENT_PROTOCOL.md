# Assessment and Evidence Protocol
## Baseline
Parent and child separately describe goals; child may opt out of individual prompts. Capture no medical/diagnostic information unless strictly necessary for safety and managed by authorized staff.
## During sessions
Mentor logs activity completed, supports offered, evidence of collaboration/problem-solving, and child enjoyment (voluntary simple check-in). Avoid hidden recording.
## Midpoint
Review attendance, child choice, guardian observations and any barriers; modify activities.
## Endline
Repeat comparable low-stakes demonstrations; review portfolio; ask child what they liked/disliked and would change.
## Evaluation
Report attendance, completion, parent-rated usefulness, child-reported enjoyment, facilitator feasibility, adverse events and economics. No causal claims from a single uncontrolled cohort.
## Safeguards
Never publish individual child profiles; guardian access controls; do not rank children or use automated profiling for admission or treatment.
