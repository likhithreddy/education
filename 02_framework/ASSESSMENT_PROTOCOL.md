# Assessment and Evidence Protocol
## Baseline
Parent and child separately describe goals; child may opt out of individual prompts. Capture no medical/diagnostic information unless strictly necessary for safety and managed by authorized staff.
## During sessions
Mentor logs activity completed, supports offered, evidence of collaboration/problem-solving, and child enjoyment (voluntary simple check-in). Avoid hidden recording.
## Midpoint
Review attendance, child choice, guardian observations and any barriers; modify activities.
## Endline
Repeat comparable low-stakes demonstrations; review portfolio; ask child what they liked/disliked and would change.
## Evaluation
Report attendance, completion, parent-rated usefulness, child-reported enjoyment, facilitator feasibility, adverse events and economics. No causal claims from a single uncontrolled cohort.
## Safeguards
Never publish individual child profiles; guardian access controls; do not rank children or use automated profiling for admission or treatment.

## Quality expansion — implementation requirements

## Observation methodology and bias controls

A dimension is an organizing concept, **not a psychometric score**. Measure concrete behaviors in a specific setting: e.g. “child named two budget trade-offs without a prompt” rather than “financial IQ 80/100.” Use a common four-level activity-specific progression: not observed; explored with support; demonstrated with support; demonstrated without a prompt in this activity. These are descriptive and non-diagnostic.

Record the scenario, facilitator prompt, available resources, role opportunity, accommodation, behavior actually seen, and child-chosen reflection. Absence or refusal is not failure. Blind double-code a small subsample and document disagreements. Compare beginning and end observations cautiously: novelty, practice, observer expectations and attendance affect results.

No leaderboards, public rankings, permanent labels, automated psychological predictions, clinical claims or sale of child profiles. Keep identifiable source records confidential; use pseudonymous exports for aggregate evaluation. Obtain guardian authorization and child assent for optional artifact sharing.

**Evaluation acceptance:** numerator and denominator for each measure, missingness, recruitment and observer bias, outcomes not observed, negative feedback, and explicit conclusion limits. A 12-week pilot can demonstrate feasibility and immediate applied behavior, not establish long-term causal impact.
