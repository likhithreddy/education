# Inclusion and accessibility guide
## Design rules
- Ask guardians privately about access needs without requesting diagnoses unless necessary for safe accommodation.
- Provide physical access audit: step-free entry, toilet, seating, emergency evacuation and transport access.
- Provide alternative participation modes: oral, written, visual, sensory-safe, seated, assisted and quiet.
- Never exclude a child based solely on disability, language, pace, neurotype or willingness to speak.
- Have specialist review of any activity requiring individualized medical or behavioral support.
- Use respectful child-preferred communication; do not share accommodations with other families.
- Budget for translation (Telugu/Hindi/English), assistive materials and support staffing.
## Session acceptance test
Every plan must specify at least one accessible alternative, a break option, a no-public-performance option and an escalation pathway.
## Open validation
Conduct accessibility walkthrough and specialist review before enrollment; do not claim universal accessibility without evidence.
