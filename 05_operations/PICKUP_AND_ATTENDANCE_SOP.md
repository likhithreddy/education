# Attendance, sign-in and authorized pickup SOP
**Before doors open:** two trained staff check exits, roster, emergency contact list and secure record storage.
1. Guardian signs child in; staff verify identity and record time; child receives non-identifying roster code.
2. Staff reconcile actual headcount with roster at session start, after breaks and before departure.
3. Child leaves only with pre-authorized adult whose identity is verified against the approved list.
4. Any last-minute pickup change must be confirmed through a previously verified guardian channel and logged; never accept a child's verbal change alone.
5. Unknown adult, missing child, incident or identity mismatch: hold handover, alert safeguarding lead, follow emergency escalation plan and document actions.
6. After departure, reconcile every child and close roster; restrict access and retention per privacy policy.
**Forms:** attendance log (date, coded child ID, arrival, departure, verifier), exception log, incident record. Do not place sensitive details in group messaging.

## Authorized child handoff protocol
**Enrollment:** verify guardian authority and approved pickup adults through a secure record; agree how emergency changes will be verified. **Arrival:** staff identify adult, record check-in time and child's pseudonymous roster ID, confirm essential accommodations and note attendance. **Transitions:** two-person check where practicable, count children when moving rooms and after breaks. **Departure:** adult proves authorization using the pre-agreed check, staff logs time and identity reference; verbal permission from an unknown number is not sufficient.

**Late collection:** remain with appropriate supervised staffing, call verified guardian through stored number, escalate after agreed threshold, never leave child alone. **Dispute:** do not adjudicate custody; use verified documentation and local advice.

**Records:** attendance retained securely with limited access. Photos of rosters on personal devices and group chat circulation are prohibited. Perform weekly exceptions audit and rehearsal prior to launch.

## Expanded operating instructions

## Child handover test procedure
Before enrollment: verify guardian and a restricted list of approved pickup adults; agree a secure method for changing authorized adults. At arrival: check adult identity against the roster, mark time and child pseudonym, confirm needs without disclosing private notes. During activity: count children at room transitions, breaks and any departures. On release: verify designated adult using stored process; record timestamp, adult reference and releasing staff member.

**Exceptions:** never accept an unexpected adult on verbal claim alone. If late pickup, keep child with appropriately supervised staff, call previously verified contact, activate agreed escalation. For custodial uncertainty, seek authorized advice without making legal judgments onsite.

**Privacy:** attendance screenshots, public messaging groups, home addresses and guardian phone lists must not be circulated. Conduct a simulated unauthorized-pickup drill before accepting children.
