# Attendance, sign-in and authorized pickup SOP
**Before doors open:** two trained staff check exits, roster, emergency contact list and secure record storage.
1. Guardian signs child in; staff verify identity and record time; child receives non-identifying roster code.
2. Staff reconcile actual headcount with roster at session start, after breaks and before departure.
3. Child leaves only with pre-authorized adult whose identity is verified against the approved list.
4. Any last-minute pickup change must be confirmed through a previously verified guardian channel and logged; never accept a child's verbal change alone.
5. Unknown adult, missing child, incident or identity mismatch: hold handover, alert safeguarding lead, follow emergency escalation plan and document actions.
6. After departure, reconcile every child and close roster; restrict access and retention per privacy policy.
**Forms:** attendance log (date, coded child ID, arrival, departure, verifier), exception log, incident record. Do not place sensitive details in group messaging.
