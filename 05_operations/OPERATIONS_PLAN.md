# Pilot Operations Plan
## Roles (minimum functions; staffing numbers require specialist/venue assessment)
Program lead; two trained facilitators; designated safeguarding lead; first-aid trained responder; admin/guardian communications; independent expert reviewers. Roles may overlap only if safe and lawful.
## Venue inspection
Safe entry/exit, toilets, accessible routes, fire exits, occupancy, ventilation, drinking water, first-aid access, pickup security, storage, food hygiene, weather contingency, child-safe furniture, landlord permissions.
## Recruitment
Written job descriptions, reference and identity checks, lawful background verification, child safeguarding training, incident reporting, signed code of conduct, backup staffing.
## Daily operations
Attendance; allergy/accessibility briefing; emergency contacts; equipment inspection; sign-in/out with approved adults; no unsupervised one-to-one adult/child contact; incident log; daily debrief.
## Vendor procurement
Three quotes where practical; contract, invoice, insurance, cancellation and safeguarding clauses; no collection of child data without approved agreement.
## Readiness drill
Simulate missing pickup, minor injury, fire evacuation, disclosure of abuse, power outage, food allergy and staff absence. Document corrective actions before launch.
