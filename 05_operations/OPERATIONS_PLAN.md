# Pilot Operations Plan
## Roles (minimum functions; staffing numbers require specialist/venue assessment)
Program lead; two trained facilitators; designated safeguarding lead; first-aid trained responder; admin/guardian communications; independent expert reviewers. Roles may overlap only if safe and lawful.
## Venue inspection
Safe entry/exit, toilets, accessible routes, fire exits, occupancy, ventilation, drinking water, first-aid access, pickup security, storage, food hygiene, weather contingency, child-safe furniture, landlord permissions.
## Recruitment
Written job descriptions, reference and identity checks, lawful background verification, child safeguarding training, incident reporting, signed code of conduct, backup staffing.
## Daily operations
Attendance; allergy/accessibility briefing; emergency contacts; equipment inspection; sign-in/out with approved adults; no unsupervised one-to-one adult/child contact; incident log; daily debrief.
## Vendor procurement
Three quotes where practical; contract, invoice, insurance, cancellation and safeguarding clauses; no collection of child data without approved agreement.
## Readiness drill
Simulate missing pickup, minor injury, fire evacuation, disclosure of abuse, power outage, food allergy and staff absence. Document corrective actions before launch.

## Implementation expansion (requires review)

## Run-of-show and responsibility matrix
**Weekly T–7d:** program lead confirms staff availability, material stock, attendance forecast, accessibility requirements and outstanding incident follow-ups. **T–48h:** facilitator rehearses scenarios and specialist-approved adaptations. **T–2h:** safeguarding lead checks site, fire access, toilets, first-aid supplies, secure pickup and hazard changes. **T–30m:** staff briefing, child pseudonymous roster, safe staffing check. **Session:** supervised attendance, breaks, staff handoffs and factual observation. **T+1h:** verified pickup completion, incident reporting, inventory, secure records and weekly cost log.

**RACI:** Founder accountable for commitments and budget; program lead responsible for scheduling; safeguarding lead accountable for child protection; facilitator responsible for instruction and behavior observations; privacy lead accountable for restricted records; independent reviewer approves age appropriateness.

**Capacity:** review real floor space, activity type, access needs, evacuation flow and break coverage before determining child-to-adult ratio. Do not advertise a fixed maximum based only on economics.

**Exception:** If safe staffing, venue, consent or pickup process is unavailable, cancel/modify the session before admission and provide parents a documented notification and remedy.

## Expanded operating instructions

## Session operations and accountable roles
**T–7 days:** program lead checks staffing, material inventory, accommodations and unresolved incidents. **T–48h:** facilitators rehearse case prompts and safe alternatives. **T–2h:** safeguarding lead verifies evacuation paths, accessible toilets, first aid, ventilation, activity materials and pickup controls. **T–30m:** staff briefing and attendance roster verification. **Session:** supervised transfers, breaks, factual observation and child choice. **T+1h:** authorized handovers, incident escalation and secure records.

**Responsibilities:** founder controls expenditures and launch; safeguarding lead has independent stop authority; program lead manages venue and roster; facilitators teach and observe; privacy officer controls child records. Safe ratios depend on activity/venue/needs and require independent review, not just budget.

**Stop operation** if staff coverage, site safety, consent, emergency readiness or authorized pickup cannot be maintained. Offer rescheduling or refund rather than improvising an unapproved location.
